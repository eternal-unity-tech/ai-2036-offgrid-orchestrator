# AI-2036 Off-Grid Human Living Devices

**Master control plane for the 50-repository AI-2036 Off-Grid Human Living Devices initiative**  
Owner: [eternal-unity-tech](https://github.com/eternal-unity-tech)

## Focus (Updated)
**Off-grid human living devices** — personal, portable, wearable, and modular devices that enable humans to live fully off-grid with AI assistance in 2036.

Core categories: personal power, water, shelter, food, health, waste, comfort, and tools. All systems are edge-AI driven, regenerative, low-energy, and designed for real-world independent living.

## Purpose
Coordinate development of 50 specialized repositories focused on futuristic AI-powered devices for human off-grid living and sustaining systems.
